# MicroBlog Privacy Principles

MicroBlog is designed around simplicity and privacy.

## Rules

1. Posts are private by default.
2. Public posts and public profiles are intentionally visible to anyone online.
3. There is no third-party analytics.
4. There is no advertising/tracking code.
5. Users only provide the minimum account information required.
6. Users confirm they are 16 or older without submitting date of birth.
7. User content is displayed as text, not rendered as HTML.
8. Database access is protected by Row Level Security.
9. Service role credentials are never exposed to the browser.
10. New tables must not be usable until explicit RLS policies exist.
11. Followers-only posts require an approved follow relationship.
